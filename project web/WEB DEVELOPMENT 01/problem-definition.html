Problem Definition

Environmental insights in Mira-Bhayandar

1

Environmental Problems

Noticing growing air and water pollution, and waste that are affecting the city's health and cleanliness.

2

Unequal Impact

Some areas of Mira-Bhayandar face higher pollution exposure and have fewer municipal services.

3

Information Gaps

Environmental data (e.g., air quality, waste collection, water quality) is not easily available or localized for the public.

4

Why Action Is Needed

To help Mira-Bhayandar become a cleaner, healthier and more sustainable city for its residents.

Project goal: Support cleaner, healthier and fairer neighbourhoods using location-based evidence.
